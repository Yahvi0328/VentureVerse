Made for students!

Real stories. Real ideas.

VentureVerse is a student-focused event by the Entrepreneurship Development Cell of JSS University, Noida. Spend a day turning early ideas into ventures, hearing how startups really begin, and discovering how entrepreneurship works right here on campus.

💡

Turn early ideas into ventures

Take a rough idea and learn the first steps to shape it into something real.

🚀

Learn from startup journeys

Founders share the wins, the pivots and the lessons they wish they’d known sooner.

🏛️

Explore entrepreneurship on campus

Meet peers, mentors and the community that backs student founders.
